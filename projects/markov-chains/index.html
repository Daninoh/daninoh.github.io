⛓️ Markov Chains Unplugged🔌
Link to Paper

Five unplugged, paper-based activities that introduce teens to Markov chains as an entry point for learning about generative language models.

Using materials that require no prior technical background other than an understanding of fractions, learners explore state-based prediction, tokenization, n-grams, and state transition diagrams, and build their own paper-based Markov chains from datasets they collect themselves.

Teaching Materials

The activities are sequential, each building on concepts from the one before. The first three include a "doing it together" section for the whole class, then an "on your own" section for individual or paired practice. Running all five takes around 90 minutes.

  1. 1

    😊 ☹️ 😊 What Comes Next?

    Learners predict the next animal emoji based on the one right before it. Spotting patterns builds intuition for how systems make predictions from prior data, and introduces tokenization.

    Prediction from prior data

  2. 2

    🐘 🦁 🐘 N-imals

    Predictions now depend on the previous two or three tokens instead of one. Learners consider how more or less context changes a model's accuracy and behavior, and the tradeoffs of memory length.

    N-gramsMore context changes predictions

  3. 3

    ☀️ 🌧️ ☁️ Sunny, Rainy, Cloudy

    Using weather emojis, learners move from single predictions to modeling a whole system, completing a state transition diagram of every possible state-to-state probability.

    State transition diagramsPrediction models as a system

  4. 4

    👊 ✌️ ✋ Rock, Paper, Scissors

    Learners generate their own datasets by playing rock-paper-scissors against each other, then think about how the quantity and quality of data shape what a model predicts.

    Data quantity and qualityBias

  5. 5

    🫩 🫠 😬 (Extension Activity) Guess the Emoji

    Bringing it all together, learners design their own emoji datasets and challenge peers to model the predictive behavior of that data using state transition diagrams.

    Dataset creationTesting
