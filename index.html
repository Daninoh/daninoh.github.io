Youth as Advisors in Participatory Design: Situating Teens’ Expertise in Everyday Algorithm Auditing with Teachers and Researchers

Daniel J. Noh, Deborah A. Fields, Luis Morales-Navarro, Alexis M. Cabrera-Sutch, Yasmin B. Kafai & Danaé Metaxa. IDC 2025.

Hi there! I am a PhD student in the Learning Sciences and Technologies program at the University of Pennsylvania working with Yasmin Kafai's research group. Our current work investigates how youth engage in the design and evaluation of AI/ML systems and how teachers engage in the participatory design of AI/ML learning activities. More broadly, I am interested in (1) interrogating relationality in participatory design and (2) privileging values of care, maintenance, and repair in design and computing education. I work closely with Yasmin Kafai, Deborah Fields, Danaé Metaxa, and Luis Morales-Navarro.

Previously, I have worked in/with various museums and makerspaces as an educator, where I designed and facilitated a collection of STEAM and design workshops, drop-ins, and media for learners of all ages and backgrounds. I have also helped develop and teach graduate courses on educational technologies, learning analytics, digital fabrication and making, learning spaces, and disability studies in design.

I hold a Bachelor of Architecture with minors in Human-Computer Interaction and Design for Learning from Carnegie Mellon University, and a Master of Education in Learning Design, Innovation, and Technology from Harvard University.
